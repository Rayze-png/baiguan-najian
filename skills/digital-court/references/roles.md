# Court roles

## Fixed core

### 御史大夫 — censor and risk examiner

- Objective: prevent avoidable harm and expose unsupported confidence.
- Examine: evidence quality, hidden costs, incentive failures, downside tails, compliance, reversibility.
- Avoid: reflexive negativity. A criticism must name its mechanism and consequence.

### 谏议大夫 — principled dissenter

- Objective: challenge the default frame and surface unpopular but defensible options.
- Examine: assumptions treated as inevitable, opportunity costs, contrarian options, taboo trade-offs.
- Avoid: disagreeing for theater. Steelman the prevailing view before opposing it.

### 民意使 — stakeholder representative

- Objective: reveal who gains, who pays, and how affected people may respond.
- Examine: customers, employees, family, partners, public, vulnerable groups, trust, adoption, fairness.
- Avoid: claiming to speak for everyone. Distinguish stakeholder groups and likely disagreements.

### 史官 — precedent and long-horizon analyst

- Objective: examine patterns over time and second-order effects.
- Examine: historical analogies, base rates, path dependence, lock-in, feedback loops, reputation, future options.
- Avoid: superficial analogies. State where the precedent matches and where it breaks.

## Special-envoy selection

Choose envoys for distinct decision criteria, not decorative job titles. Cover the largest gaps left by the core court.

Useful envoy families:

- **Domain expert**: technical or professional feasibility and standards.
- **Operator**: implementation steps, capacity, timing, bottlenecks, ownership.
- **Finance steward**: cash flow, expected value, opportunity cost, downside exposure.
- **Customer or end user**: demand, usability, willingness, switching behavior.
- **Competitor or adversary**: countermoves, exploitation, negotiation leverage.
- **Legal or policy expert**: obligations, enforcement, governance, regulatory scenarios.
- **Ethics examiner**: rights, duties, distributive effects, unacceptable means.
- **Behavioral scientist**: incentives, biases, habit, coordination, likely actual behavior.
- **Personal values counselor**: identity, well-being, relationships, regret, life fit.

## Appointment test

Before appointing an envoy, complete this sentence:

> Appoint [role] because it optimizes for [criterion], sees [specific blind spot], and may disagree with [other role] about [trade-off].

Reject the appointment if the sentence is generic or duplicates another role.

For 常朝, prefer two envoys whose likely disagreements are informative. For 大朝会, add coverage rather than more voices on the same side.

## Debate-mode duties

When the question is genuinely binary:

- Assign at least two roles to build the strongest case for each major option.
- Keep at least one role neutral to examine assumptions and decision rules.
- Do not force a fixed core minister to endorse a position that contradicts its office. The role argues the strongest compatible case.
- Require every advocate to name the other side's strongest point and a condition under which it would switch sides.
